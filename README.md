## Controls
- JK - Adjust the horizontal axis zoom 
- UI - cycle through the colors
- H - Turn off graph follow mode
- WS - move traverse up and down the graph if graph follow mode is off
- SPACE - Pause the game

## How to Play
Upon booting up the trading system, you will be displayed the stock graph of the DEATH CAPITAL index fund concerning all death related companies. You will see the stock price axis to the right of the graph, with the number of days elapsed at the bottom axis. Below the stock graph is the events bulletin, here you will be displayed different events which may display information about the socio-economic status of the world or events that can effect the game. You will see a log of all events that happened by pressing the log button and using the arrow buttons to scroll through it.

At the right side of the GUI displays your balance, shares, value of your shares, previous price you bought the stock at, and the current price of the stock. Directly bellow th stock information displays the buy and sell controls where you can choose how many stocks you want to buy and how much it costs. You can also press the /10 and x10 buttons to adjust how much stock you want to buy.
